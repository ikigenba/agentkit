# Phase 52 — Anthropic Claude 5 models: fable-5, sonnet-5

*Realizes design Decision 16 (the anthropic Claude-5 slice). Depends on Phase 8 (the `anthropic` sub-package and its registry/pricing/reasoning-spec seam).*

The `anthropic` sub-package's model registry gains two new entries — `claude-fable-5`, `claude-sonnet-5` — each co-located with an exported constant (`anthropic.ModelFable5`, `anthropic.ModelSonnet5`) and carrying both its `Pricing` and its `ReasoningSpec` verbatim from the D16 tables. Pricing is a single base `RateTier` per model with the Anthropic cache-write 5m/1h buckets populated: fable-5 10000/1000/12500/20000/50000, sonnet-5 3000/300/3750/6000/15000 (the standard $3/$15 sticker, not the introductory promo). Each reasoning spec is an effort enum `[low, medium, high, xhigh, max]` with `Default=Level("medium")` (AgentKit's chosen warn-fallback value, per D16); the two differ on disable behavior — `claude-fable-5` is `CanDisable=false` (thinking always on), `claude-sonnet-5` is `CanDisable=true`. The additions are purely additive — the existing opus-4-8/sonnet-4-6/haiku-4-5 set is untouched — and are picked up by the registry-wide golden and completeness tests (supported ⇒ priced ⇒ reasoning-describable) with no new adapter wiring.

**Done when:** R-CH7P-Y5OU (both constants are exported and each prices at the D16 rates including the cache-write buckets — sonnet-5 at the sticker output rate 15000, not the intro) and R-CIFM-BXFJ (both reasoning specs are the effort enum `[low,medium,high,xhigh,max]` with `Default=Level("medium")`, and they differ on `CanDisable`: fable-5 false, sonnet-5 true) are each covered by a clearly-named test, and the suite is green per D16 Conventions.
